# RETAIL-SALES-ANALYSIS-DASHBOARD


**Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop | **Skills:** DAX, Data Modeling, Data Visualization, Business Insights

## Project Overview
An interactive, multi-page Power BI dashboard that analyzes retail sales performance across 2024 and 2025. It covers about 25,000 transactions and gives management a clear view of sales, discounts, products, customers, and employees across 7 Nigerian cities.

## Objectives
- Track key sales KPIs in one executive view
- Identify top-performing products, categories, employees, and customers
- Compare sales performance across cities and departments
- Understand monthly sales trends and discount impact

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Dashboard
Six headline KPIs, a monthly sales trend, written insights, and slicers for Year, Store, Category, and City.

| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | $614M |
| Total Transactions | 25K |
| Quantity Sold | 74K |
| Average Sales | $24.81K |
| Discount % | 7% |
| Total Discount | $49.6M |

### 2. Employee Report
- Sales and transactions by employee
- Sales and quantity sold by department

### 3. Customer Report
- Sales, transactions, and quantity sold by customer
- Sales and customer count by city
- Sales by gender

### 4. Product Report
- Sales, transactions, and quantity sold by product
- Sales and transactions by category
- Customers by product

## Key Insights
- **Sales performance:** $614M in total sales from about 25,000 transactions, with an overall discount rate of 7%.
- **Categories:** Beauty ($167.08M, 27.23%) and Home ($162.47M, 26.48%) contribute the largest shares. Fashion, Electronics, and Grocery follow.
- **Top products:** Dove Soap ($29.94M) and Binatone Microwave ($29.76M) lead in sales. Binatone Microwave also has the most transactions (1,206).
- **Monthly trend:** Sales fluctuate through the year, peaking in August and May and dipping in February, which shows growth is not consistent.
- **Employees:** Grace Okafor is the top performer, with $9.23M in sales and 353 transactions. By department, Operations leads with $173.1M (28.21%), followed by Sales ($167.21M).
- **Customers:** Peter Ali and Tunde Eze are the top customers at $2.46M each. Linda Okafor made the most transactions (98).
- **Cities:** Sales are evenly spread across cities, led by Enugu ($64.69M, 14.91%) and Abuja ($63.12M).
- **Gender:** Male customers contributed $310M and female customers $303M, a nearly even split.

## Data Model
Star-schema model with a central sales fact table connected to customer, product, store, employee, and date dimension tables.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Data modeling with relationships between fact and dimension tables
- DAX measures for KPIs, discounts, and totals
- Interactive dashboards with slicers and page navigation
- Dashboard design and storytelling with data
